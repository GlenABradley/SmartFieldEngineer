# Code of conduct

Smart Field Engineer welcomes thoughtful technical collaboration. Participants are expected to make the project safer, clearer, and more useful while respecting the people doing the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive, especially when challenging a design or result.
- Critique ideas and observable behavior rather than people or motives.
- State uncertainty, distinguish evidence from opinion, and correct mistakes openly.
- Respect privacy. Never share customer data, credentials, private paths, recovery material, or another person's private information.
- Honor the project's reviewed contract and owner-decision process; disagreement is welcome, misrepresenting adoption or validation is not.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and other abusive conduct are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing this project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through a private channel listed on their GitHub profile. Do not put sensitive personal details in a public issue. When no suitable private project channel exists, use GitHub's platform reporting tools.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently exclude participants whose behavior conflicts with this policy. Reports should be handled as privately and impartially as practical.
