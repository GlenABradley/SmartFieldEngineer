## What is settled

- Three commercial buyers: premium independent, developing independent, service-business sponsor.
- Roles: owner, dedicated technician, pool technician, integrator dispatcher; these do not establish technical competence.
- Expert mode can skip instructional sequencing; required evidence and authority gates remain.
- Guidance has its own execution/evidence/supervision policy, not a theme on an expert interface.
- Routine work proceeds under standing policy; approval prompts are for authority gaps and material exceptions.
- Protected envelopes include mentor attention where required.
- Local/cloud routing is normal task selection, not forced local failure; local 7B is not the architecture.
- Multi-home isolation gates shared use; it does not block single-operator identity work.
- Fit-pricing is policy and economics, separate from model routing; the model does not own commercial commitment.
- Field Nation actions require real account entitlement and confirmed outcomes.
- Refurbished hardware and low-cost diagnostic instrumentation remain the first-round posture.
- Fiber workflows, fusion splicing, robotic execution and custom carrier development remain halo.
- The $10,000 seat is a pricing hypothesis; the 240–480 hour estimate is a focused pilot estimate, not full-brief development.
