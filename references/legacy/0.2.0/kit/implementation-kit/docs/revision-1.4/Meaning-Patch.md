## Chapter 2 insert

[FLOOR] **Business Home Role:** scoped authority held by a person in one Home; owner, dedicated technician, pool technician and integrator dispatcher are distinct assignment/permission profiles, not buyer tiers or skill ratings.

[FLOOR] **Capability Opening:** the supported starting experience offered to a buyer after setup; opening capabilities do not confer technical qualification or bypass the release gate for a shared service.

[FLOOR] **Local Ledger Namespace:** a persistent identity for one trusted local data store, used to disambiguate records and survive backup/restore; it is not an authenticated Business Home or tenant-isolation boundary.

[FLOOR] **Expert Mode:** permits direct action selection and omission of instructional sequencing; it cannot omit required evidence, change the authorized scope, or bypass authority controls.

[HORIZON] **Guided Mode:** a distinct instructional/evidence execution policy tied to assignment and graduation limits, with mentor capacity and exception escalation; it is not merely a visual theme for Expert Mode.

[FLOOR] Role grants, entitlement, competence and assignment are evaluated separately; a high technical grade does not grant AR access and an owner role does not establish technical competence.
