# Chapter 2 — revised terms, insert-ready overlay

[FLOOR] Revision 1.3 supplements Operational Chapter 2 without renaming its existing vocabulary; the original definitions continue except for the explicit extensions below.

| Label | Existing term | Preserved meaning and explicit extension |
|---|---|---|
| FLOOR | Business Office | Commercial and administrative coordination within one active Business Home; role and account context accompany every action |
| FLOOR | Workshop | Technical workspace governed by observed capability, current site reality and authority; instrumentation and physical action are never inferred from a model response |
| FLOOR | Astra / coordinating intelligence | Conversational coordination across Office and Workshop, implemented by evaluated local/cloud components; no prescribed model size or implied autonomous authority |
| FLOOR | Agent employees | Named responsibilities, not claims of separate minds or independent commitment authority |
| FLOOR | Job Dossier | Persistent sourced job history bound to a Business Home, with linked immutable asset binds, approvals, events and independent completion tracks |
| FLOOR | Current Job Reality | Sourced present account of facts, uncertainty, capabilities and permission; unresolved conflict remains visible and model memory is not asset evidence |
| FLOOR | Operations Knowledge Base | Approved contextual references; Home-private knowledge remains isolated and platform applicability is explicit |
| HORIZON | Field Mission Harness | Existing job-specific procedure plus distinct expert and guided execution policies, with per-step evidence gates and supervised escalation |
| FLOOR | Procedural Precedent | Reviewed experience with original context; it cannot override present facts or become another Home's data without permission |
| FLOOR | Approval Aperture | Authority bound to exact action, scope revision, Home, actor/role, recipient, limits and expiry; material changes require revalidation |
| FLOOR | Portfolio-level scheduling | All commitments and personal blocks affect availability; cross-home conflict checks can consume private busy intervals without disclosing job details |
| FLOOR | Reserved operational envelope | Protected work and surrounding obligations, now explicitly including required mentor attention and escalation capacity |

| Label | New term | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| FLOOR | Business Home | An isolated business authority and data boundary; a person can hold distinct roles in several Homes |
| FLOOR | Commercial Seat | A priced entitlement with declared user, device, software, support and service limits; neither a bag nor a bill of materials |
| HORIZON | Apprenticeship Graduation | An owner-approved, evidence-backed change to scoped capability, permitted commercial band, and supervision demand together |
| HORIZON | Platform Pack | Versioned semantic procedure and verification knowledge bound to a vendor family, models and tested firmware; not a string replacement dictionary |
| HORIZON | Voice Bridge | A Home-scoped Dossier briefing bound to an evidenced live call session and an explicit participation mode |
| FLOOR | Asset Bind | An immutable revision linking a verified identity observation to a Home-scoped asset; serial recall uses the bind, not inference |
| FLOOR | Fit-pricing | Eligibility and economics evaluated together against demonstrated capability and the full operational envelope, with controlled commercial authority |
| FLOOR | Instrument Capability | A typed, versioned measurement/action contract declaring scope, limits, evidence, calibration and actual availability independently of executor type |
| FLOOR | Floor / Horizon / Halo | Baseline requirement, gated expansion, or fenced exploration, respectively; these do not replace implementation or validation status |

[FLOOR] Existing party distinctions remain: technician, owner, marketplace, buyer, client and site contact are not interchangeable, and site access does not grant purchasing or scope-change authority.

[FLOOR] Existing record distinctions remain: opportunity, assignment, estimate, quote, purchase order, invoice and reconciled payment describe different states and different evidence.
