## Three buyers and multiple homes

[FLOOR] Glen confirmed three buyers spanning the market: premium independent, developing independent, and service-business sponsor of technician seats, with opening capabilities matched to the buyer rather than separate product engines.

| Label | Buyer | Opening capability target | Expansion condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| FLOOR | Developing independent | Scoped guided work, explicit evidence checks, mentor escalation, basic office records | Demonstrated skills and owner-approved graduation expand eligible jobs and pricing while reducing supervision |
| FLOOR | Premium independent | Expert technical workspace, job continuity, delegated office work within standing authority, quiet exception handling | Release-qualified integrations expand execution without forcing beginner guidance |
| FLOOR | Service-business sponsor | Assigned seats and jobs, technician roles, isolated customer work, supervisor oversight and scoped evidence review | Validated Home isolation and role controls precede any shared deployment; additional seats do not bypass release gates |

[FLOOR] These opening capabilities are commercial entry requirements, not claims of present prototype functionality; the current kit remains a single-operator local foundation.

[FLOOR] Buyer category, purchased entitlement and demonstrated competence are separate fields: paying for a higher tier cannot graduate a technician or confer network-change authority.

[FLOOR] A person can belong to multiple Business Homes and work through several marketplaces; the active Home, acting role, bill-to identity, Job Dossier, account connection, and approval context must be explicit before every action.

[FLOOR] Isolate records, attachments, caches, retrieval indexes, model context, secrets, usage charges, exports, phone routing, and background work by Home; a shared device, person, or serial does not authorize cross-home data reuse.

[FLOOR] A technician's cross-home availability service may expose opaque busy intervals under delegation, but must not reveal another Home's customer, price, contacts, or job content.

[FLOOR] Imported cross-home evidence requires a recorded grant specifying fields, purpose, recipient, expiry and source; revocation prevents future access while already-issued records retain a controlled audit history.

[FLOOR] The current single-operator SQLite prototype has none of these Home isolation controls and must not be represented as a multi-home product.

## Graduation and fit-pricing

[HORIZON] Each graduation record binds technician, skill, Platform Pack version, assessed task scope, observed jobs, independent reviewer, limits, required supervision, commercial band, review date and revocation triggers.

[HORIZON] Proposed progression is supervised steps, checked milestones, exception-led work, and independent operation within a demonstrated scope; passing one platform does not graduate another.

[HORIZON] Graduation is an explicit owner decision supported by field evidence, never a self-rating by the model or a count of completed screens; incidents or material platform changes can restore more supervision.

[FLOOR] Capability eligibility, suggested price band, and reserved owner-attention minutes update in one approved graduation revision so a cheaper-looking job cannot conceal expensive supervision.

[FLOOR] Price recommendations account for complete travel/work/closeout envelopes, materials, fees, risk, supervision cost, verified capabilities and owner floors; market demand can inform a quote but cannot certify competence.

[FLOOR] The owner chooses commercial terms or delegates bounded rules; the model proposes and explains fit and price, while deterministic authority controls any submitted offer.

## Voice Bridge contract

[HORIZON] A bridge session binds Home, Dossier revision, authenticated participants, call-leg identifiers, mode, disclosure/consent state, event timestamps, connection evidence, and a concise sourced briefing.

[HORIZON] Listen-along means an authorized connected participant receives the agreed audio while their microphone is muted; hot join means the authorized participant is connected with two-way audio and introduced to the caller.

[FLOOR] Never display “Glen is listening” or “Glen has joined” on the strength of a dial request or a prepared Dossier; provider connection evidence plus the appropriate participant acknowledgement is required.

[HORIZON] Changing modes requires explicit control and verified state; interruption changes presence to disconnected or unknown and preserves the callback obligation.

[FLOOR] Recording, transcription, summaries and live participation are separate permissions and capabilities; a bridge may carry live audio without retaining a recording.

## Copy and acceptance law

[FLOOR] Product copy must not say “literally do anything,” “dumb hands perfect splice,” “the AI handles the ecosystem,” or “the model is bidding the jobs.”

[HORIZON] The product promise remains a professional business around the engineer, and supported guided work around a developing technician, with every operational claim tied to its actual evidence and release status.

[FLOOR] Release tests must cover wrong-Home reads and actions, stale approval after graduation or pack changes, serial correction with historical exports, unsupported firmware, lost voice legs, denied instrument capabilities, and a fit-price that cannot cover required supervision.

[FLOOR] These are acceptance obligations, not tests executed in this revision.
